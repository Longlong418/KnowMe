name: weekly-brief
description: Prepare a weekly or morning brief from the user's calendar, mail, tasks, and recent memory.
---

## Instructions

1. Gather the calendar window, important mail, open tasks, and recent relevant
   memory using the available read-only tools.
2. Start with a short focus summary: the two or three things that matter most.
3. Group the rest into schedule, messages, tasks, and risks or follow-ups.
4. Include concrete dates and times, with the timezone when one is known.
5. Separate facts from suggestions and link each action to its source when the
   tool provides one.
6. End with a small proposed plan for today or the next working day.

## Edge cases

- If a source is unavailable, say which source was unavailable instead of
  inventing content.
- Avoid repeating unchanged items from an earlier brief.
- Keep the result concise enough to scan in under two minutes.
